Distinguish measured data, transferred estimates and illustrative values. Keep actual AI-assistance provenance for the eventual disclosure.

Use these four documents as the canonical records, avoid duplicate content and repair links when reorganizing. Keep credentials, temporary captures and build outputs out of commits. Routine authorized edits and synchronization need no repeated confirmation. Repository access changes, public publication and messages to collaborators are not part of this recording task.
